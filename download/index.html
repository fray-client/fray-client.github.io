  1. Open the installer

    It's Fray-Beta-Installer.exe, in your browser's downloads or your Downloads folder.

    Make the AppImage runnable first: right-click it, Properties, Permissions, allow running it as a program. Or in a terminal: chmod +x Fray-Beta-Installer-x86_64.AppImagechmod +x Fray-Beta-Installer-aarch64.AppImage

    Windows says it protected your PC? Click More info then Run anyway.

    Browser won't keep it? Chrome: Keep. Edge: ... › Keep › Show more › Keep anyway.

  2. Sign in with Microsoft

    Use the account you play Minecraft on. You sign in on Microsoft's own page, so your password never reaches Fray.

  3. You're in, or on the list

    Testers: press Install the beta and you're playing. Not in yet? You're on the waitlist. Open the installer again once you're in.
